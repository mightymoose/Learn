## Who

The learner is **Carla**, not Ryan. Every other subject in this repo teaches Ryan.
Address lessons to Carla. Pitch them at a beginner who has opened Excel but has never
been taught it.

## Why

Carla wants to stop being blocked by spreadsheets at work. Today a colleague sends her a
file and she can read it, but she cannot change it without fear of breaking it. She wants
to go from reading other people's spreadsheets to building her own, and to be the person
her team asks.

## Success looks like

- Take a raw export of a few thousand rows and answer a question about it in ten minutes.
- Write a formula that still works after somebody adds 500 more rows.
- Build a summary that updates by itself when the source data changes.
- Open an unfamiliar workbook, find the formula behind a number, and explain it.
- Say why a number is wrong, not just that it is wrong.

## Route

Four tiers, beginner to expert. The order is a dependency chain, not a difficulty ramp.
Each tier assumes the one before it. The full ladder is the roadmap card at
[reference/0000-course-roadmap.html](./reference/0000-course-roadmap.html), which is the
page Carla should open first.

1. **Foundations.** The grid, references, tables, aggregation.
2. **Working analyst.** Lookups, logic, text and dates, PivotTables, charts.
3. **Building models.** Dynamic arrays, model layout, auditing, scenarios.
4. **Expert.** Power Query, the data model and DAX, LAMBDA, automation, performance.

## Constraints

- **Excel version is unconfirmed.** Tier 1 avoids every feature that varies by version,
  so the written lessons hold on any Excel from 2016 onward, Windows or Mac. Tier 2 and
  later fork on this. Ask Carla before writing them. See [NOTES.md](./NOTES.md).
- Carla learns by doing, so every lesson names a file to open and a thing to try.
- No deadline is known. Lessons favour long-term retention over coverage speed.

## Out of scope

- VBA as a programming course. Automation appears in tier 4 as one lesson, no more.
- Excel as a database. If the answer is a real database, the lesson says so.
- Certification exam preparation. No exam is known to be driving this.
